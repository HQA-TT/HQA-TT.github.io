ARTVIO - PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 29 October 2026
Effective date: 29 October 2026


1. WHO WE ARE

Artvio ("the App") is developed and operated by ArtemisX ("we", "us", "our").
This policy explains what information the App collects, why we collect it, who
we share it with, and what choices you have.

Contact: support@artemisx.net
Website: https://artemisx.net

By installing or using Artvio you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not
agree, please do not use the App.


2. SUMMARY

- Artvio has no accounts. You do not register, and we do not ask for your name,
  email address, or phone number.
- Photos you choose and text you type are sent to our servers only to produce
  the result you asked for, and are then forwarded to a third-party AI provider
  that performs the processing.
- Content used for generation is not stored on our servers, except when you
  voluntarily submit selected AI content in a report. We also keep aggregate
  usage counters (number of requests and tokens per day).
- The App shows advertising. Advertising partners collect device and
  advertising identifiers in order to serve and measure ads. This is the main
  category of personal data associated with the App.
- Images you create and your chat history are stored on your device. If you
  choose Report, the selected AI answer or generated image is sent to us for
  review together with your report details.


3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

3.1 Information you actively provide

- Photos. When you pick a photo to transform, the App resizes it and sends it
  to our processing servers. You choose each photo through the Android system
  photo picker; the App does not browse or scan your photo library.
- Text prompts. Descriptions you type to generate an image are sent to our
  processing servers.
- Chat messages. Messages you send in the AI chat feature are sent to our
  processing servers so a reply can be generated. Because the AI service is
  stateless, the current conversation is re-sent with each message.

- Content reports (optional). When you tap Report on an AI answer or image,
  we receive that selected answer or a resized copy of the generated image,
  your chosen reason, any comments you enter, app version, selected language
  and a random report reference. We do not attach your entire chat history,
  photo library, email address or a device identifier to the report payload.
  Reports go directly to Artvio for review, not to our AI provider or analytics
  services. Standard server connection logs may contain your IP address.

Do not include personal, confidential, or sensitive information in photos,
prompts, chat messages or report comments.

3.2 Information collected automatically

Through the third-party services listed in section 5, the following may be
collected:

- Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID).
- Device information: device model, manufacturer, operating system version,
  screen characteristics, language, country, time zone, mobile network and
  connection type.
- App information: app version, install and first-open time, install referrer
  (which store campaign led to the install), session start and duration.
- IP address, which also gives an approximate (city or country level)
  geographic location.
- Ad interaction events: ad impressions, clicks, and ad revenue.
- In-app events used to measure performance, such as feature usage counts.

3.3 Information we do NOT collect

- No account, name, email address, phone number, or password.
- No precise (GPS) location. The App does not request location permission.
- No contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera access.
- No reading of your photo library. The App only receives the specific photos
  you select in the system picker.
- No health, financial, or biometric data. Faces in photos you submit are
  processed as ordinary image data to produce a stylised image; we do not
  perform biometric identification and do not build face templates or profiles.


4. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

- To provide the features you request: transform your photo, generate an image
  from your text, and answer your chat messages.
- To display and measure advertising, including rewarded ads that unlock image
  creation.
- To measure marketing performance and attribute installs to advertising
  campaigns.
- To understand how features are used so we can fix problems and improve the
  App.
- To enforce usage limits and protect the service against abuse and automated
  misuse.
- To investigate content reports, moderate offensive content and improve
  content safety and filtering.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. ADVERTISING AND YOUR ADVERTISING ID

Artvio is free and supported by advertising. Some features require watching a
short rewarded video ad.

Advertising partners may use your advertising identifier to show ads that are
more relevant to you and to measure ad performance. You can control this in
Android:

- Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads
  Personalisation). Ads will still appear, but will be less relevant.

Where required by law, a consent prompt is shown before personalised ads are
used, and you may change your choice at any time.


7. DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION

Our servers
- For generation requests, photos, prompts, and chat messages are held in
  memory while the AI provider processes the request. They are not written
  to disk by our proxy, except for selected content you voluntarily report.
- We retain aggregate usage counters: number of requests and tokens per day,
  and per anonymous client identifier. These counters contain no personal content.
- Voluntary content reports are stored separately on our servers with access
  restricted to authorized reviewers. Reports are automatically deleted in
  daily cleanup after 90 days of inactivity. We use them to investigate
  issues and improve moderation. Contact support@artemisx.net for assistance
  with a report or to request deletion; provide the approximate date and type
  of content so we can locate it without an account.
- Standard server logs may briefly record IP addresses and request paths for
  security and troubleshooting.

Third parties
- Advertising, analytics, and attribution partners retain data according to
  their own policies, which are linked in section 5.


8. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The following is stored on your device. AI processing and voluntary reports
send only the content described in section 3:

- Images you have created (visible under "My Creations").
- Your chat history.
- Your language selection, theme, and onboarding state.

Automatic cloud backup is disabled for this App, so this data is not copied to
Google Drive and is not transferred to a new device.

To delete all of it, uninstall the App or use Android Settings > Apps > Artvio
> Storage > Clear data. Images you have explicitly saved to your gallery remain
in your gallery and must be deleted there.


9. CHILDREN

Artvio is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 years old, or 16 in
the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, to use the App. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child
has provided us with personal information, contact us at support@artemisx.net
and we will take appropriate action.


10. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete,
or restrict processing of your personal information, to object to processing,
to data portability, and to withdraw consent.

Because Artvio has no accounts, we usually cannot connect a request to a
specific person from our side. The most effective controls are:

- Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
- Clear the App's data or uninstall the App to remove everything stored
  locally.
- Contact the relevant advertising or analytics partner directly using the
  links in section 5.

You can also write to support@artemisx.net. To help us act on a request
involving advertising data, include your advertising ID.

If you are in the EEA or UK, the legal bases we rely on are: performance of a
contract (providing the features you request), consent (personalised
advertising, where required), and legitimate interests (security, abuse
prevention, and improving the App).

If you are in California, we do not sell or share personal information as those
terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.


11. SECURITY

Traffic between the App and our servers uses HTTPS. Requests are signed so that
our servers can reject requests that do not originate from the App, and rate
limits are applied to prevent abuse. No method of transmission or storage is
completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.


12. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Our service providers operate globally. Your information may be processed in
countries other than your own, including countries whose data protection laws
differ from those of your country.

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and
the current version will always be available at
https://hqa-tt.github.io/artvio/privacy.html. Significant changes will be announced
